Jyotirmoy Chakraborty · IIT Guwahati

My path into research

I started in Mechanical Engineering at Tezpur University, where I became interested in how computational methods and physical systems can work together. My undergraduate project used computer vision, camera calibration and edge hardware to monitor distances in real time.

During my MS by Research at IIT Guwahati, I began focusing on battery management and state estimation. Battery systems are a compelling research problem because the most important internal states cannot be measured directly, and practical control decisions must remain safe even when models are imperfect.

My current work combines equivalent-circuit and thermal modelling, local estimation, selective cloud communication and real-time validation. I am especially interested in making AI-assisted engineering systems more interpretable and reliable.

Research direction

Going forward, I want to connect data-driven estimation with physical constraints, mathematical reasoning and experimental evidence — especially across changing batteries, operating conditions and embedded hardware.
