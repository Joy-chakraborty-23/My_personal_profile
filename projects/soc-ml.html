Problem & motivation

SOC cannot be directly measured and must be inferred from available battery signals.

02 / CASE STUDY

My contribution

Developed a modelling and evaluation workflow to compare FCN, CNN and LSTM approaches, including data processing and hyperparameter optimization.

03 / CASE STUDY

Method and implementation

LG HG2 battery measurements → time-series preprocessing → neural models → Optuna tuning → MSE/MAE evaluation and prediction plots.

Limits and future work

RESEARCH DESIGN

Question, model and validation method

Research objective

Compare data-driven SOC estimation methods under a common battery-data processing and evaluation workflow.

Computational formulation

Supervised time-series regression using FCN, CNN and LSTM neural architectures for SOC prediction; MSE and MAE are evaluation criteria.

Technical deep dive: assumptions, data and reproduction ↗

Dataset and evaluation setup

LG 18650HG2 battery data; original source: Kollmeyer et al. (2020), Mendeley Data, DOI 10.17632/cp3473x7xv.3.

Baselines and comparisons

FCN, CNN and LSTM model families are described as comparators in the repository README; exact split protocol and benchmark numbers need further independent review.

What reproduction requires

Consult the linked repository for scripts and processing steps; check the data license and original Mendeley dataset conditions before reproducing results.
