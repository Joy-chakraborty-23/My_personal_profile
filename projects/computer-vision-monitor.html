01 / CASE STUDY

Problem & motivation

Monitoring spacing in crowded scenes requires identifying people and correcting perspective distortion.

02 / CASE STUDY

My contribution

Built person detection, perspective calibration and a custom 3D-printed camera mounting setup.

03 / CASE STUDY

Method and implementation

YOLOv5 detections → perspective transform → physical-distance estimation → visual risk indication.

04 / CASE STUDY

Results and evidence

CV reports 89% detection accuracy in crowded indoor environments and 60% lower hardware cost from a custom mount; experimental protocols are not available in the supplied files.

05 / CASE STUDY

Limits and future work

Accuracy and cost figures are CV-reported rather than independently reproduced in this portfolio. Evaluate domain shift, camera calibration robustness and privacy-preserving inference.
